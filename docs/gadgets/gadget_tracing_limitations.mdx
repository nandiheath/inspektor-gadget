---
title: Gadget tracing limitations
sidebar_position: 1
---

This guide covers known limitations of gadget tracing in Inspektor Gadget.

### Statically compiled binaries

Uprobe-based gadgets, such as `trace_malloc`, cannot trace statically compiled
binaries. They also cannot trace binaries that invoke syscalls directly using
`syscall(syscall_id, ...)`, bypassing the standard C library wrappers that
uprobes hook into.

### I/O performed through `io_uring`

Gadgets that trace I/O operations, such as `trace_open`, rely on hooking
individual syscalls (e.g., `open()`). These gadgets cannot observe operations
that are submitted through `io_uring`, because `io_uring` bypasses the normal
syscall entry path and dispatches requests directly within the kernel.
